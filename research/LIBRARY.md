### ESP32-PICO-V3 symbol: in-package flash and NC pins

- Pin names, numbers and types from Table 4 (p18-20), checked number-by-number against KiCad's stock
  `MCU_Espressif:ESP32-PICO-V3` (0 name differences). Power -> power_in; VDD_SDIO (26, "output power supply") ->
  power_out; EN and I34-I39 -> input; I/O -> bidirectional; LNA_IN -> passive (RF into the match, as on
  `lib:ESP32-D0WD-V3`).
- In-package flash (Table 5 p21, Table 7 p22, Fig 8 p49): CMD/IO11 (30) = FLASH_CS and CLK/IO6 (31) = FLASH_CLK are
  exposed; they keep their datasheet type (bidirectional) and are named `CMD/IO11_FLASH`, `CLK/IO6_FLASH`, grouped at
  the bottom left; Fig 11 (p52) leaves them open ("cannot be used for other functions") -> no-connect flags in P4.
  GPIO16/17/18/23 drive the flash inside the SiP and are not exposed, so they have no pin.
- NC (Table 4 "No connection"): 25, 35, 36, 44, 45, 47, 48 -> type no_connect, name NC (BOM-REDUCTION §6 item 1 lists
  25/35/36/44/45; Table 4 also marks 47 and 48 NC on the V3, former CAP2/CAP1 of the D4).
- Description carries the rules: VDD_SDIO open (Fig 11), MTDI/IO12 low at reset (3.3 V flash), -40..85 °C.
- Fields: Manufacturer Espressif, MPN ESP32-PICO-V3, LCSC C967022, Datasheet (Espressif URL), Footprint.

### ESP32-PICO-V3 footprint decisions to review

- Mask: pads 1-48 copper-defined, local mask margin +0.05 (= Fig 16 opening 0.78 x 0.35; web 0.15, corner web 0.15
  with the rounded openings); EP mask margin 0 (the 4.50 opening defines the joint once the EP sits in the GND pour =
  Espressif's solder-mask-defined thermal pad).
- Paste: EP 16 x 0.75 mm apertures exactly as Fig 17 = 44.7 % (spec `ratio_min` 0.44 instead of the checker's 50 %
  default). Pads 1-48 paste 1:1 with the copper; Fig 17 adds a 0.15 mm outward overprint (7.71 overall) for an 80 um
  stencil, not used at the 0.2 mm body spacing (a 0.10 mm stencil gives the same volume).
- EP vias: not in the footprint (as the D0WD and SX128x footprints); Fig 16 shows 21 x 0.25 on a 1.05 / 2.00 grid,
  P5 places them as filled+capped 0.35/0.20 via-in-pad (D34).
- NC pads kept (LGA attachment; trimming them saves no courtyard area).
- 3D: no LGA model from Espressif; the easyeda2kicad model for C967022 was rejected (z -0.18..0.53, 0.71 mm vs A 0.94).
  KiCad stock `QFN-48-1EP_7x7mm_P0.5mm_EP5.6x5.6mm.step` (CC-BY-SA 4.0, 7.0 x 7.0) copied in as
  `LGA-48_7x7mm_P0.5mm_EP_ESP32-PICO.step`, z scale 0.7833 (1.2 -> 0.94 mm); render: pin-1 dot at pad 1
  (`p23/bomred_lib/render/pico_top3d.png`).
- DRC on a scratch board next to copies of the project `.kicad_pro` / `.kicad_dru`, every pad on its own net (PICO +
  SOT883): 0 violations. 0.5 mm pitch is above NextPCB's 0.38 mm fine-pitch floor: no EQ, no DRU exception.

## Changes made in the merge

1. Symbols: 19 merged verbatim (`merge_sym.py`), then KiCad re-save. Footprint fields set to the assigned variant
   for TPS22810DBVR (SOT-23-6 _Dense) and TPS7A2018PDQN (X2SON-4 1x1 _Dense). LP5907SNX-2.85 LCSC field emptied (it
   held a sentence; no LCSC listing, bom lcsc stays null). `lib:U.FL-R-SMT-1(80)` shares its name with the OpenDrone
   symbol; the nickname keeps them apart, and J1 now uses the lib one (typed pins, MPN fields, lib footprint).
2. Footprint assignment rule: `_Dense` where a staging group made one for the area budget, else `_W` where the
   OpenDrone original fails its datasheet check, else the new footprint. U12 TPS7A2018 follows U22 LP5907 onto
   `X2SON-4_L1.0-W1.0-P0.65-TL-EP_Dense` (same TI DQN0004A drawing, same X2SON1 1.1 mm budget; the fc group had only
   made `_W`). J20-J22 (LED BZ+ BZ-) use `SolderPad_1.0x1.2mm_Dense` (1.0 x 1.0, spec 4.10).
3. The 17 OpenDrone footprints bom_plan used without library work (C/R/L 0201, C 0402, C 0603, both crystals, TCXO,
   QFN-60, LGA-14, SC70-6, WSON-6, SOD-123FL, DFN-3L, DEA filter, AOTA inductor, XL-1010 LED) all FAIL
   check_footprint against their datasheet lands (wrong land sizes/positions, no F.Fab body, oversized or missing
   courtyard; `p23/merge/od/check_originals.txt`). Each got a `_W` copy from the datasheet land in the same frame, and
   a `_Dense` copy where the land exceeds the budget envelope (`spec/budget.py`) and the toe can be trimmed safely
   (9 parts). All 26 PASS; specs and sources in `p23/merge/od/gen_od.py`.
4. D1 SMF5.0A (unidirectional) moved from `Device:D_TVS` (bidirectional, pins A1/A2) to `Device:D_Zener` (1 = K,
   2 = A): pad 1 is the cathode and the bom nets list +BATT_IN first, so polarity is now explicit.
5. R67 lcsc filled with C60485 (vtx group screened it for RC0402JR-070RL).
6. `check_footprint.py`: spec `"pin1": null` accepted for single-terminal footprints (pin 1 n/a; a marker can
   never be nearer pin 1 than its mirror when the only land is centred), and an `"artwork"` mode for the copper-less
   logo (no pads, polygon count, artwork bbox, courtyard). Both proposed by the io_pads group.
